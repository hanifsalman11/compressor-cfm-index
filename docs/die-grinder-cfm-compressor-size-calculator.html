Frequently Asked Questions (Die Grinder)

How many CFM and what size compressor do I need for Die Grinder?

A standard continuous pneumatic die grinder draws 11.5 CFM @ 90 PSI. Factoring in a common 75% production duty cycle (8.63 CFM) and a mandatory 1.25x engineering safety factor, you need an air compressor producing at least 10.8 CFM @ 90 PSI. To support this delivery rate and avoid motor overheating, pair the tool with a 60-gallon tank driven by a 3.7 to 5.0 HP continuous-duty electric motor.

How long will a 60-gallon air tank run Die Grinder before refilling?

Based on Boyle's Law (P1V1 = P2V2), a 60-gallon tank (8.02 cu ft) cycling between a 150 PSI cut-out and a 90 PSI cut-in provides an effective reserve volume of approximately 32.74 standard cubic feet (SCFT) of air. When powering a die grinder pulling 11.5 CFM continuously, the tank delivers approximately 171 seconds (2 minutes and 51 seconds) of full-throttle operation before the compressor pump must engage.

What size air hose and fittings should I use for Die Grinder?

Use a minimum 3/8-inch inner diameter (ID) air hose for runs up to 50 feet, or a 1/2-inch ID hose for runs exceeding 50 feet, to avoid excessive friction loss. Additionally, replace restrictive standard 1/4-inch industrial (I/M) couplers with Milton V-style high-flow fittings, which increase air delivery by up to 70% and prevent dynamic pressure drop at the tool's motor inlet.
