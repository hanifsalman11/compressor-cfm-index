Frequently Asked Questions (Much Do I Need Air Compressor)

How many CFM and what size tank do I need for Much Do I Need Air Compressor?

The Much Do I Need Air Compressor draws 11.5 CFM continuously (8.6 CFM at a 75% work cycle). To prevent waiting for the tank to pump up, use a 60-gallon (3.7 HP) compressor delivering at least 10.8 CFM at 90 PSI.

How long will a 60-gallon tank run Much Do I Need Air Compressor before dropping below 90 PSI?

By Boyle's Law, a 60-gallon tank dropping from 150 PSI to 90 PSI releases 32.7 cubic feet of free air. Without the motor running, that reserve alone powers a 11.5 CFM load for 171 seconds—after which your compressor pump motor must keep pace with the 8.6 CFM demand.

Why does my Much Do I Need Air Compressor lose power after 15 seconds even when the tank gauge reads 120 PSI?

That is caused by restrictive 1/4-inch hoses and standard 1/4" industrial M-style quick-disconnects, which bottleneck airflow to ~6–8 CFM. Switching to 3/8-Inch ID Hybrid Polymer hose and 1/4" High-Flow V-Style (or 3/8" body) couplers restores full torque and RPM.
