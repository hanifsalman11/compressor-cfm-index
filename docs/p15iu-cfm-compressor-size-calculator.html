Frequently Asked Questions (P1.5Iu)

How many CFM and what size compressor do I need for P1.5Iu?

The P1.5Iu draws 11.5 CFM at 90 PSI continuously; factoring in an industrial 75% duty cycle and a 1.25x safety margin requires a compressor rated for at least 10.8 CFM @ 90 PSI. A 60-gallon tank paired with a 3.7 HP motor is recommended to prevent short-cycling and motor burnout.

How long will a 60-gallon air tank run P1.5Iu before refilling?

Using Boyle's Law across an operating delta of 150 PSI cut-out to 90 PSI cut-in, a 60-gallon receiver yields approximately 32.7 standard cubic feet of usable air buffer. At a continuous consumption rate of 11.5 CFM, this provides roughly 171 seconds of uninterrupted run time before the pump must cycle on.

What size air hose and fittings should I use for P1.5Iu?

You should use a 3/8-inch or 1/2-inch inner diameter (ID) hose equipped with high-flow Milton V-style or Euro-style 1/4-inch fittings. Standard industrial ARO or M-style couplers create severe dynamic restriction at 11.5 CFM, causing up to a 15-20 PSI drop at the tool inlet.
